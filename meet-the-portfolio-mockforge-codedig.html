Meet the Portfolio, Part Four: MockForge and CodeDig

Meet the portfolio, part four: MockForge and CodeDig.

Two developer tools from the same engineering team behind everything else here. Both are working products, not just ideas.

MockForge

MockForge is open-source service virtualization across 10+ protocols: realistic backends for development, QA, and integration teams, with no live services required. Spin up the API you wish existed and test against it.

  • Open source and installable, with an admin UI for managing virtual services.
  • Record-and-replay and AI-assisted data synthesis for realistic test traffic.
  • Built for teams blocked on dependencies they do not control.

Visit MockForge →

CodeDig

CodeDig scores every pull request for risk and blast radius, so reviewers catch the scary merges before they ship. It plugs into the review workflow teams already have and points attention where it matters most.

  • Risk scoring on every PR, with blast-radius analysis for large changes.
  • Reviewer focus: spend time on the merges that can actually hurt you.
  • Tiered plans from a free tier up through team and enterprise.
