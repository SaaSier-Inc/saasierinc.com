Capability Statement

SaaSier Inc is a woman-owned small business providing business operations consulting and applied AI services, delivered through its operating subsidiary, SaaSy Solutions LLC.

Company Overview

SaaSier Inc is an Indiana S-corporation, 100 percent owned by Macon Wright, Founder & CEO. Its operating subsidiary, SaaSy Solutions LLC, has delivered client services since 2023, helping small and mid-sized teams replace fragile manual work with practical automation. Engagements are scoped, senior-led, and defined with success metrics upfront.

SaaSy Solutions is also the provider of Hello SaaSy, an AI business operations platform covering CRM, projects, finance, payroll, and compliance, with AI assistance built into daily workflows.

Core Competencies

  • Process Automation and Workflow Optimization (NAICS 541611, 541618). Audit of manual processes, redesign, and automation of the work between systems, so information is entered once and moves on its own.
  • Custom Systems Development and Integration (NAICS 541511, 541512, 541519). Custom software and integration across the tools a business already runs, including API development and data movement between platforms.
  • Intelligent Customer Experience (NAICS 541512, 541618). Customer-facing workflows, intake, and follow-up systems that respond faster and drop fewer requests.
  • Predictive Analytics and Decision Intelligence (NAICS 518210, 541519). Reporting and forecasting that turn operating data into decisions leadership can act on.
  • Data Intelligence and MLOps (NAICS 518210, 541512). Data pipelines, model development, and production machine learning operations, built and run by senior engineers.
  • Intelligent Search and Discovery (NAICS 541519, 518210). Search and knowledge retrieval over a company's own documents and records, including retrieval-augmented AI systems.
  • Hello SaaSy Platform (NAICS 513210, 518210, 541519). Hosted AI business operations platform with CRM, projects, finance, payroll, compliance, and AI assistance in one system.

Differentiators

  • Senior-led delivery. Work is led by operators and senior engineers, including CTO-level production AI and SaaS engineering experience of 25+ years. There is no handoff to junior staff after the sale.
  • Scoped engagements with metrics defined upfront. Every engagement states the outcome, the measure, and the timeline before work starts.
  • Operator perspective. The firm was built by a CEO who ran operations herself; recommendations come from having done the manual work, not from theory.
  • Federal-ready small business. SAM.gov registered, woman-owned, and Indiana based, with NAICS coverage across management consulting, software, systems design, and data services.

Past Performance

Client services have been delivered through SaaSy Solutions LLC since 2023, across business modernization, HR and workflow infrastructure, process automation, and custom systems work for small and mid-sized businesses, under commercial services agreements.
