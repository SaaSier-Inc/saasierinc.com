# SaaSier Inc — Brand Guidelines (2026)

These guidelines summarize the SaaSier Inc Brand Kit 2026. The complete brand guide,
logo files, social templates, and platform specifications are available in the full kit
(`SaaSier_Brand_Kit_2026/`) and the downloadable brand guide PDF.

## 1. Brand Identity & Voice

SaaSier Inc is not another software product. It is the company that creates, owns, and
scales specialized technology businesses. The identity is enduring, structured, and
investor-ready — more durable than any individual portfolio company.

**Positioning:** Building the future of focused software.

**Voice:** Direct, intelligent, grounded, and owner-minded. Lead with what the portfolio
builds and why it matters. Avoid vague innovation language such as "revolutionary,"
"disruptive," or "cutting-edge" unless the statement is supported by evidence.

**Corporate descriptor:** A women-owned technology holding company.
**Portfolio endorsement:** "A SaaSier Inc company."

## 2. Color Palette

- **Midnight Navy** `#071A36` — primary authority and trust. Headlines, dark bands, footers.
- **Founder Gold** `#D4AF4A` — ownership, value, and momentum. Accents only, never a
  background default.
- **Operating Slate** `#334155` — supporting text and structure.
- **Cloud** `#F3F4F6` — calm backgrounds and separation.
- **White** `#FFFFFF` — clarity and breathing room.

**Recommended ratio:** roughly 65% white/cloud, 25% midnight navy, 10% founder gold.
Gold is an accent, not decoration — this keeps the company premium and credible.

**Accessibility:** gold fills always carry navy text (white on gold fails contrast).
For small text and icons on light backgrounds, use the darker bronze ramp of gold.

## 3. Typography

- **Display:** Inter Display SemiBold / Bold (on the web: Inter variable with optical
  sizing, which renders as Inter Display at heading sizes).
- **Body:** Inter Regular / Medium / SemiBold.
- **Microsoft 365 fallback:** Aptos. **Universal fallback:** Arial.

Use bold, tightly written headlines and highly readable body copy. Avoid futuristic
"AI" fonts, excessive gradients, or playful product-style typography at the
parent-brand level. Inter is available from Google Fonts.

## 4. Logo System

The mark is a dynamic, forward-leaning "S" formed by two interlocking elements that
suggest movement, building, and elevation — SaaSier Inc bringing distinct companies
together with shared systems, capital, and operating discipline.

- **Primary wordmark:** horizontal lockup (gold mark + navy SAASIER INC) for light
  backgrounds. A reversed version exists for navy/dark backgrounds.
- **Monogram / one-color marks:** for avatars, favicons, and constrained spaces.
- **Clear space:** keep at least the height of the gold mark around every side.
- **Minimum size:** 120px wide for the wordmark, 24px for the monogram.
- **Never:** stretch, rotate, add shadows, alter proportions, or recolor individual
  components outside the approved palette.

## 5. Visual Language

Depth comes from muted navy-and-gold ombre over a warm ivory canvas, a faint tiled
S-mark pattern, and generous white space — not from glassmorphism, gradient text, or
decorative effects. Dark sections are midnight navy with a soft founder-gold glow.
Photography and product artifacts should feel practical and operational, matching the
brand pillars: Focused, Connected, Durable, Ambitious.
