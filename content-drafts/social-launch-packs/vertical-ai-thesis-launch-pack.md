## Core angle

A founder note on why SaaSier Inc chose vertical AI for beekeeping over building horizontal tools — domain depth, economic weight of the industry, and foundation models making the AI layer practical for the first time.

## LinkedIn post

```text
"Why build a whole platform for beekeeping?"

It's the question I hear most often when people learn about Hive Minded AI. Here's the honest answer in three parts.

First, horizontal farm management platforms can't go deep enough. They track inventory and log tasks but they can't tell you that a specific brood comb photo matches a Varroa mite threshold, or that Tuesday's weather forecast means moving an inspection window. That requires domain models — colony lifecycle, disease progression, treatment intervals, pollination math. Horizontal tools will never build those because the economics don't pencil out for a module.

Second, beekeeping carries more economic weight than most people realize. Pollination-dependent crops are about a third of US food supply. Almonds alone need 2+ million hives every February. Those hives travel thousands of miles on trucks. Meanwhile the software available to a commercial operator hasn't improved much since 2010 — spreadsheets, paper notebooks, disconnected sensor dashboards.

Third, AI changes the equation. Foundation models let us build visual disease detection, natural language inspection dictation, and predictive health alerts from a single AI layer, fine-tuned on beekeeping-specific data. That wasn't possible five years ago without a dedicated ML team.

The full note goes deeper on the thesis and what it means for how we build and hire.

https://saasierinc.com/founder-note-vertical-ai-thesis.html
```
