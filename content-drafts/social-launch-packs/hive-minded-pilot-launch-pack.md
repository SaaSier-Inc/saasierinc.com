## LinkedIn post

```text
Our beekeeping AI platform just crossed a real milestone — Hive Minded AI is now in private pilot with five commercial operations across Indiana, Ohio, and Michigan, covering roughly 2,400 hives.

The pilot covers the workflows operators tell us cost them the most: structured colony inspections (with voice dictation for gloved field work), AI-assisted disease detection, weather-aware task planning, and per-apiary business analytics.

Most beekeeping ops still manage data across spreadsheets, paper notebooks, and disconnected sensor dashboards. Building a unified platform for a niche vertical like this only made sense once foundation models made the AI layer practical without a dedicated ML team.
```
